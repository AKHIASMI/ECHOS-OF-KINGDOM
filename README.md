# Echoes of the Forgotten Kingdom

*A 3D fantasy adventure of forgotten ruins, magical crystals, ancient
puzzles, and encroaching darkness.*

## About the Game

The kingdom has fallen, and only its echoes remain in ruined courtyards,
forgotten sanctuaries, and the grounds surrounding an ancient castle.

In **Echoes of the Forgotten Kingdom**, players explore a mysterious
fantasy world, solve puzzles, collect magical energy crystals, discover
clues from ancient tablets, and fight to survive supernatural threats.
Each level reveals more of the forgotten kingdom and challenges the
player to complete objectives before time runs out.

Developed with **Unity 3D** and **C#**, the project demonstrates
interactive 3D game development, player movement, camera control,
collectible systems, puzzle interactions, and damage mechanics.

## Game Levels

  Level     Name                    Time Limit   Number of Demons
  --------- --------------------- ------------ ------------------
  Level 1   Forgotten Courtyard           2:00                  1
  Level 2   Ruined Sanctuary              1:30                  2
  Level 3   Castle Outside                1:00                  3

The first two levels take place inside the kingdom. The third and final
level takes place outside the kingdom, in an environment containing
trees, roads, lamps, rocks, and other outdoor features.

## Gameplay Features

### Explore the Kingdom

Travel through ancient courtyards, ruined sanctuaries, castle grounds,
and outdoor terrain filled with mysterious objects and forgotten
history.

### Collect Energy Crystals

Find and collect **three energy crystals** to unlock the gate and
progress through the kingdom.

### Solve Ancient Puzzles

Discover clues and interact with ancient tablets and objects to complete
objectives and reveal the path forward.

### Avoid Demons

Each level introduces a different level of danger. Players must avoid or
fight supernatural threats while managing their remaining time and
health.

### Use Safe Zones

Safe zones provide a temporary refuge from demons. Demons cannot enter
these areas, and the player can enter one to avoid danger and regenerate
health.

### Activate the Portal

Light up the **Sun, Moon, and Stars** to activate the portal
automatically. The player can enter the portal after completing all
required objectives.

## Technologies Used

### Unity 3D

Unity is the game engine used to create the 3D fantasy world. It
supports environment design, lighting, visual effects, camera systems,
physics, collisions, and interactive gameplay.

In this project, Unity is used to build the ancient castle, terrain,
player character, magical crystals, cursed areas, safe zones, and other
interactive objects.

### C

C# is the programming language used to implement gameplay logic in
Unity.

Project scripts are used to manage mechanics such as:

-   Player movement and camera control
-   Player interactions with objects
-   Collectible energy crystals
-   Gate and portal activation
-   Puzzle hints and objectives
-   Damage zones, health, and safe-zone behaviour
-   Demon-related gameplay interactions

## Objective

Explore the fallen kingdom, gather the required energy crystals, solve
the puzzles, survive the demons, activate the portal, and complete every
objective to advance through the levels.

## Installation and Running

This project is developed with Unity. To run it from the source:

1.  Install a compatible version of **Unity Hub** and the **Unity
    Editor**.
2.  Obtain or clone the project repository.
3.  In Unity Hub, select **Add project from disk** and choose the
    project folder.
4.  Open the project with its compatible Unity version and allow the
    assets to import.
5.  Open the game's starting scene, then press **Play** in the Unity
    Editor.

If a built game executable is provided, run it directly on a supported
platform. Actual controls and platform requirements should be documented
here once finalized.
